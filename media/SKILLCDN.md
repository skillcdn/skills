# Rules for media skills

These add to the rules of the repository.

**A story holds from the viewer's seat.** Where a skill tells a story, who knows what, and since when, is written down for every character and for the viewer before anything is produced, and every line, reaction and twist agrees with it. A character never reacts to what they have not seen.

**Fiction stays fiction.** Characters, places and events are invented. No real person's name, face or voice, no real brand, and no one else's footage goes into a prompt or a result. Generated people are delivered as generated: the delivery says so, and the platform's label for AI-made content is the user's to set.
