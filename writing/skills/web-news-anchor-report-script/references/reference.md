# A reference: the form of a report the user names

What a report the user names gives the script, how it is read, and what of it enters no file. Read at the start of phase 2, only when the user named one.

## What it gives

Its form and nothing else: how long the lead runs before the handover, the order of its parts, how its sentences end, how many causes or sides it tells in full, how it closes, what the screen shows under each part, where sources are named on screen, and its pace.

## How it is read

Its script where the user pastes one, its captions where the host can read them, and its screen from frames where a browser is at hand ([video-frames.md](/docs/browser/video-frames.md)); where none can be read, ask for the script. Its pace is counted as [script.md](script.md) "Length" counts the script's own, the spoken syllables or words over its seconds, and is used in place of the default.

## What stays out

Its story, its people, its sentences and its outlet's name are not form: none enters the script, the cut sheet or any file the run keeps, the notes on its form included. Write the form as measures and orders (seconds, counts, the order of the parts, the kinds of ending), never as its sentences quoted. The checkpoint says in a line what was taken from it.
