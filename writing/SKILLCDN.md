# Rules for writing skills

These add to the rules of the repository.

**The writing set comes first.** A text is written by the pages of [`/docs/writing/`](/docs/writing/README.md), read before the first sentence: the reader and the register, the sentences, the facts, what leaves the organization, the house style. A skill reads the pages its runs need before it writes, included with it where its size allows and linked from the phase that writes where it does not, keeps its hard rules in its own form, and repeats no other sentence of the set.

**As firm as its sources.** A text states nothing its material does not hold, attributes what someone said to who said it, and says what is still to be confirmed in its own words; the user sees the text itself before it goes anywhere.
