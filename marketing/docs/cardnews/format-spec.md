# The format spec

The format spec is what a card-news account's posts look like, measured and written down so that a new post can be built in the same shape: the canvas, the ways the account builds each kind of post, each slide type with its anatomy, the elements that ride on slides, the text density, the register, the caption conventions, what the account never does, and the nearest fonts and colors. The study skill writes it as a JSON file (`format-spec.json`) beside a short notes page (`style-notes.md`); the build skill reads both before it plans a single slide. The example JSON the study skill ships is the shape; this page says what each field means, and a field an account needs and the shape lacks is added and named in the study's delivery. Numbers in this page are illustrative; the spec carries the measured values, and marks as an estimate anything read by eye rather than counted. A position in an anatomy is a block's edges as percentages of the canvas (top and bottom always, left and right where the block does not span the margins), because the build places every block by them and its review compares them on the PNG.

## Fields

| Field | Holds | Obtained from |
|---|---|---|
| `account` | The handle studied, the day it was read, how many posts were visible without signing in and how many were read. | The profile. Stays in the user's spec; never in a skill's files. |
| `categories` | The kinds of story the account posts, each with its share of the visible posts and one example line (a caption's first line, quoted to identify the post). | The profile grid and the post objects of the visible posts. |
| `cadence` | Posts per day or per week and the hours they go out, counted from the visible posts' times, pinned posts left out. | The post objects. |
| `canvas` | The ratio of a slide measured on the post pages, the platform's documented width and supported ratios with the day they were looked up, the size that follows (width over ratio), and the slides per post as a range with a typical count. | The post pages; the platform's help pages. |
| `kinds` | Each kind of post the account makes (a story, a list, an event, a single image, a video) with the builds it uses for it: each build's slide count, what carries the text (sentences on photos, short boxed lines, the caption alone), the slide types in a typical order, and the posts it was seen in. | The posts read, grouped by kind and build. |
| `cover` | The anatomy of the first slide: photo treatment, the label (its kind of words, color, weight, italic or not, position), the headline (lines, characters per line, weight, color, style), and `variants` for covers that differ (a list cover, a sponsored cover, a cover with a source card). The cover is described here and not in `slide_types`. The label's words are the account's series name and never enter a build; its look does. | Two or more covers, side by side. |
| `slide_types` | One entry per slide type the account uses other than the cover, each with a name, the anatomy, the content it carries, the posts it was seen in, and `tentative` when seen in one post only: a story slide (photo, one-line sub, three lines of body), a photo-only slide, a collage, a lead slide, a poster slide, an information slide. | Every slide of the posts read, grouped by what repeats. |
| `elements` | What rides on slides of other types, each with its anatomy, the slide types it rides on, how often, and the content it carries: a serif note, an inset (an album cover, a poster, an archive photo as a rectangle on the photo), a talk box (a white box of one or two short lines), a source card, a source tag. | The `other` column of the slide log. |
| `density` | Per slide type and element, and for the cover's headline: lines, characters per line, sentences, characters per sentence, sub characters, where detail goes (the caption), one fact per sentence or not. Counts (lines, sentences) as the most common value and the range; lengths (characters) as the median, the middle half and the range. | Counted on the slides, in the account's script. |
| `register` | How the slides, the notes, the talk boxes and the caption are each written, as patterns with what sounds wrong beside what sounds right: headline style, sub style, body endings, the voice, the phrasings that would not appear. Named in the devices of the account's language. Where posts of one kind differ, both with their counts and the content each went with, the majority as the default. | The slides and the captions read side by side; described in English, the account's own sentences not copied. |
| `caption` | The order of the caption's parts (facts with a date and a source, context, a closing line, the credit line, mentions, an information block for an event), the credit line's exact pattern, the information block's pattern, and whether hashtags appear. | Three or more captions. |
| `variants_by_content` | Which slide type or element the account uses for which kind of content (a list, a date, a quote, a number, a mood, a reaction, the thing a sentence names), so that the build chooses by content rather than by habit. | Where each variant appeared and what it carried. |
| `never` | Statements of what the account does not do, each naming the kind of post it holds for and how many of that kind were read: a call to action on a story post, numbers as overlays, text on every slide. Only from a kind read two or more times. | Absence across the posts read. |
| `fonts`, `colors` | The nearest free web fonts for the display, the serif and the label faces, with where to load them and the observed weights and width, each marked `guess` when identified by letterforms; the label color, the text color, the gradient's darkness and the dim overlay as hex or rgba, each marked `estimate`. | The slides at zoom. |
| `posts_read` | The post ids read slide by slide with their kinds, builds and slide counts, so that a later run does not re-read them and a research knows what the account has already covered. | The run. |

## How the build reads it

The build takes `canvas` and the build of `kinds` that fits the post as its frame, `density` and `register` as the checks every line passes, `cover`, `slide_types` and `elements` with `variants_by_content` as the catalogue it picks layouts from, `caption` as the template of its caption, and `fonts` and `colors` as its CSS tokens. A value marked `estimate` may be adjusted where a render shows it unreadable, and the build says so. A field missing from the spec is derived from the build skill's own defaults and said so at the plan checkpoint.
