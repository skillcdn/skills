# Rules for marketing skills

These add to the rules of the repository.

**Claims come from the brand.** Every statement about a product, brand or service comes from its own material (its site, its documents) or from the user. Story, setting and characters are fiction and read as fiction.

**Assets come from the brand.** Logo, type, colors, images and people come from the brand's own material or from what the user supplies. Reference material (someone else's ad, post or video) is studied for why it works and never reused in the result.

**The result fits its channel.** Format, length, aspect ratio and caption conventions follow what the target platform documents today, discovered at run time rather than assumed.
