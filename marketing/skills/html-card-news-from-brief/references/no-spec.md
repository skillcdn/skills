# With no format spec: the stand-in

Read only when the user wants slides and has no reference account to follow. This is not a format; it is the least a carousel needs so that a user with no account in mind sees something and asks for the study next. Every checkpoint and the delivery say the post was built on this page and not on an account's format.

- Canvas: the platform's documented portrait size, looked up at run time (1080 by 1350 for 4:5 at the time of writing, illustrative).
- Two slide types. A cover: a full-bleed photo, a gradient over its lower part, a headline of two lines in a heavy free sans that covers the post's script, inside side margins of about a fifteenth of the width, its block ending about a ninth of the height above the bottom. A text slide: a full-bleed photo with the same gradient, a one-line heading in the heavy weight and up to three lines of body in the regular weight at about a forty-fifth of the height, bottom-left inside the same margins. A photo-only slide is the text slide with no text.
- Fonts: one free sans for the post's script with a regular and a heavy weight, loaded from its distributor's documented path, named at the plan checkpoint.
- Colors: white text; a gradient from transparent to about 80% black where the type sits.
- Density: a headline line of about twelve characters in a script of syllable blocks and about twenty-five in a Latin script; body lines of about thirty in a script of syllable blocks and about thirty-five in a Latin script, since a line of forty syllable blocks wraps in the stand-in's column at its body size; counted before setting, as script.md says.
- Register: the stand-in names none, so the slides use the post's language in short polite sentences (in Korean the ~요 and ~세요 endings) with noun endings for labels, said at the plan checkpoint, unless the user names a voice.

Nothing here is measured on an account; the study replaces all of it.
