# The studio: a repository that keeps producing

A studio is the repository in which a team and its agents keep turning out one kind of thing, or keep running one kind of round, day after day: card news for an account, ad creatives every week, the episodes of a series, the rounds in which running ads are tuned; or two kinds, where one works on the other. It is three things together. The first is the layout every repository of this pack has ([layout.md](layout.md), [agreement.md](agreement.md), [console.md](console.md), [testing.md](testing.md)), laid by `git-repo-setup`. The second is a small pack of its own, in which the studio's skills extend the pack's and what the studio knows is kept as pages an agent reads little of ([studio-know-how.md](studio-know-how.md)). The third is this page: the units and their states, the ledger, the screen a person reviews on, the commands, the check, and how several people and agents work in it at once. Read when a studio is set up, and before a skill adds a kind of file to one.

## What a studio adds to the tree

```
SKILLCDN.md             the studio's manifest: its name, its description, the rules of every job here
studio.json             the units: each kind's folder, its states, which of them a person gives, its ledger
skills/<job>/SKILL.md   one skill per job done here, each extending a skill of the pack
docs/policy.md          the person's standing answers, edited in place
docs/guides/<step>.md   what the studio knows about one step of the work, as it holds now
docs/trials.md          techniques tried: against what, with what result, what was decided
formats/<name>-v<n>/    the design as files; a change that alters finished work is a new folder
<folder>/_template/     the folder a new unit starts as
<folder>/<slug>/        one unit in progress: its record, its inputs, its outputs, its review record
<folder>/<filed>/<day>/<slug>/    the same folder once it reached the last state
<ledger>                one row per unit of a kind: what was made or tried
scripts/                new, file, gallery, serve, check, and studio.mjs, which they share
gallery/                generated by the gallery command, never committed
.cache/                 what a run fetches or installs for itself (a browser driver, a downloaded video), never committed
```

`docs/guides/`, `docs/trials.md` and `formats/` appear with their first content, as layout.md has it for every file. The state flow and the commands are described for people in `docs/architecture.md`, and nowhere else.

## The kinds of unit

A unit is one thing made or one round of ongoing work, and its folder, its record and its states have the same shape whichever it is.

| Kind | A unit is | Its folder holds | Its ledger row says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Something made | One post, one creative, one episode | What it is made from, what was rendered, the reviewer's record | What it is about, so that nothing is made twice |
| A round of ongoing work | One change to something that runs, read after a set time: a budget moved, an audience narrowed, a creative swapped | What was changed and why, the time it is read, the readings, what was decided | What was tried on what, so that nothing is tried twice blind |

Most studios hold one kind. A studio holds two where one works on the other: creatives made every week and the rounds in which the running ones are tuned are one repository, since a round names the creatives it changes and the next creative is written from what the rounds found. Each kind has its own entry in `studio.json`, its own folder, template, states and ledger, and they meet in one field: a record names the units of the other kind it is about by slug, in a field called as that kind's folder (a round's `creatives`). The review screen links the two both ways, and the check fails on a slug that names nothing. A second kind costs a session nothing it does not load: a job's skill names its own unit, the agreement routes by job, and a job reads the other kind only through the slugs its unit names. Two kinds that never name each other are two studios.

## studio.json

`units` is a list with one entry per kind, a list of one for most studios; `limits` stands beside it.

| Field of a unit | Holds |
|---|---|
| `unit`, `folder` | The unit's name in one word (`post`, `creative`, `round`) and its folder (`posts`). The record file is `<unit>.json`. |
| `states` | The states in order, the first being the one a new unit starts in. `archived`, for a unit that will not go on, is always there, is not listed, and is set on the person's word. |
| `person` | The states only a person gives (an approval, a decision). A record at or past one holds `<state>: { "at", "by" }`, written by whoever received the person's answer, with their words. |
| `filed` | The last state. A unit in it is moved to `<folder>/<filed>/<day>/<slug>/`, the day being that of `<filed>.at` in its record. |
| `ledger` | The ledger's path, each kind's its own. |
| `needs` | Per state, the files a unit's folder must hold from that state on. |
| `previews` | Paths inside a unit's folder, with `*` in the file name, of what the review screen shows, pictures or video (`out/*.png`, `out/*.mp4`); the first that matches anything is used. Left out for a round. |
| `shows` | The record's fields the review screen prints beside the title: a round's change, its readings in a line, its decision. |
| `large` | The subfolders of a unit whose files go to large-file storage (`img`, `out`). |

`limits` holds the character bounds the check keeps, named only where a default does not fit: `AGENTS.md` 12,000, `SKILLCDN.md` 3,000, `docs/policy.md` 8,000, `skill` (each studio skill) 8,000, `page` (each page of `docs/`) 16,000.

## A unit's record

`<unit>.json` holds `slug` (the folder's name, `YYYY-MM-DD-words-in-kebab-case`, the day being the one the unit is meant for), `title`, `status`, `owner` (one worker at a time), `task` (the board task), `created_at`, `format` (a folder under `formats/`, where the unit has a design), `notes`, and what the states add: the object of each person state, the object of the filed state with its `at`, and `<state>_for` for a state that waits for a time (`scheduled_for`, or `running_for`, the time a round is read). A time is written with its zone. Where the studio holds two kinds, the field called as the other kind's folder holds the slugs this unit is about.

A unit moves forward one state at a time, each written by whoever did the work of that state. A unit changed after a person saw it goes back to the state before theirs. Only the person's word sets a person state, a go-ahead included: a run that may go ahead alone still stops there.

## The ledger

A Markdown table that ends its file, one per kind, one row per unit from the moment it is started: `| slug | state | what | keys | who | note |`. `what` is the unit in one line. `keys` are two to four specific names of what is made or tried (a person, an organization, a thing, an act; for a round, the object and the change), never a word as broad as the studio's field. `who` is the subject. A new unit that holds a key of another row is refused, and made all the same only on the person's word, which the row's `note` then carries. The state cell is never written by hand: the file command rewrites it from the record.

The ledger is the index of everything finished. An agent that needs to know whether something was made or tried reads the ledger, never the filed folders.

## The review screen

The gallery command writes `gallery/index.html` from the records, and the serve command shows it at a local address, built again on every visit. Each kind has its section: first the units that wait for a person (those one state before a person state), then every other open unit by state, then the filed ones by day. A card shows the first preview (a video by its first frame), the title, the state, the owner, the task, the fields `shows` names and the slugs of the other kind the unit names. A unit's own page shows every preview, a video with its controls, the units it names and those that name it as links, each Markdown record of its folder (`review.md`: one line per look, saying what was looked at and what was found or fixed; a round's `readings.md`) and the record whole.

A person is asked to approve or to decide with the screen's address or with the previews themselves, on the board where the repository has one. Their answer is written into the record by whoever received it. The screen writes nothing.

## The commands

Small scripts without dependencies, run from the root through the task runner (`npm run <name>`), shipped as text by `git-studio-setup` and owned by the studio once copied. `scripts/studio.mjs` holds what they share.

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `new [<unit>] <slug> --title --what --keys [--who --task --format --allowed]` | Copies `_template` to the unit's folder, fills the record with the first state, the owner and the day, and adds the ledger row. The unit's name comes first where the studio holds two kinds, and an option called as the other kind's folder (`--creatives="<slug>, <slug>"`) records what the unit is about. Refuses a slug that exists and a key another row holds, unless `--allowed` carries the person's words. |
| `file` | Run after any change of state. Rewrites the ledgers' state cells from the records, and moves each unit in its filed state to its day's folder with `git mv`, rewriting the paths in its JSON files that climbed to the root. |
| `gallery`, `serve` | The review screen, and the local address that shows it and every file of the repository, nothing cached. |
| `check` | Below. |

What makes the thing (a renderer, an exporter, a measuring script, a sync of readings) is the making skill's, added under `scripts/` when the first unit is made and then the studio's own.

## The check

One command, the same before a commit and in continuous integration, where it needs no binary: a large file may be a pointer, so the checkout fetches none.

- **The configuration and the records:** each kind's name, folder and ledger its own; the folder's name against the slug's shape, the record's `slug` and `status`, the files `needs` names from each state on, the person's `at` and `by` from a person state on, the format's folder, and a unit for every slug a record names of the other kind.
- **The ledger:** a row for every unit, its state equal to the record's, its `what` and `keys` filled from the first person state on, no key held twice without a note.
- **Storage:** every file under a `large` subfolder is tracked by git LFS.
- **The pages:** every relative link of every Markdown file resolves; the pages every session reads stay within `limits`; every page of `docs/` is linked from another page; `AGENTS.md` names every folder under `skills/`.
- **Warnings**, which a session reads before anything else: a unit in the filed state that is not filed, and one whose `<state>_for` has passed while it still stands in that state. Until `AGENTS.md` and `docs/README.md` are written, the routes and the orphan pages are not checked, and a warning says so for each; from then on both fail the check.
- **A kind's own rules** are the default export of `scripts/check-<unit>.mjs`, called with that kind's units where the file exists: a caption's length, a slide's fact, at most one round running on one market.

A second job of continuous integration runs the indexer's own check on the studio as a pack (studio-know-how.md "The checks").

## Working together

- **One unit, one worker.** A unit's folder is changed by its `owner` alone. A parallel worker started by a session writes its own unit's folder, commits nothing and returns to the session that started it.
- **What goes where.** A change inside one unit's folder, with its ledger row, goes straight to the main branch after the check: folders do not collide. Formats, scripts, `studio.json`, the studio's skills and its pages are changed in a commit of their own, through a pull request where the team reviews, and never in the middle of a batch: a worker that wants one changed says so in its return. A commit that changes a format or a script names the units that render differently and were rendered again.
- **The files everyone touches** (the ledgers, `AGENTS.md`, the lockfile) are edited small and rebased right before the push. A row of a ledger is added by the new command and its state by the file command, so two sessions rarely touch the same line.
- **A session starts** with `console whoami`, a pull (with `git lfs pull` where it will look at previews), the check, whose warnings are dealt with first, then the task and the studio skill of its job. It ends with what the run learned folded back (studio-know-how.md "Folding work back"), the file command, the check, the commit and the push. The agreement's Workflow says this in the team's words, and no other page restates it.
- **A session that runs on a schedule** (a morning look, a filing after a booked time) commits to the main branch, so its checkout is left on that branch.
- **Who decides what** is the policy's Approval line. A decision is asked of that person, on the board where there is one.

## Starting values by kind

The plan starts from the row that fits and changes it with the user; a request that names two kinds takes both rows as two units of one studio, with the field in which one names the other. Only the first row is the shape of a studio that ran for weeks; the others are derived from the pack's skills.

| Studio | `unit`, `folder` | `states`, the person's in bold | Keys name | The pack skills its skills extend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A card news account | `post`, `posts` | idea, brief, draft, review, **approved**, scheduled, published | The event: its people, organizations, things, acts | `web-card-news-topic-brief`, `html-card-news-from-brief`, `html-card-news-batch`, `instagram-post-scheduling`. The paths, the policy's headings and the states are those of the card news set's [account record](/docs/card-news/account.md), followed as written |
| Ad creatives | `creative`, `creatives` | idea, script, draft, review, **approved**, launched | The product, the hook, the reference it started from | `meta-ad-library-creative-research`, `higgsfield-shorts-ad`, `meta-ads-setup` for the launch |
| A series of short videos | `episode`, `episodes` | premise, script, draft, review, **approved**, published | The premise, the characters | `higgsfield-shorts-drama` |
| Ad optimization | `round`, `rounds` | planned, running, read, **decided** | The object (a market, a campaign, an ad set) and the change | `meta-ads-setup`, its monitoring phase with [monitoring.md](/engineering/docs/meta-marketing-api/monitoring.md): a round's change is one a rule there proposes, `running_for` is the end of the rule's evidence window, `readings.md` holds the site's figures with the platform's beside them for that window, and the decision (keep, revert, scale, pause) is the person's. What the policy lets an agent do inside a running round without asking (a pause at a bound it states) is an action, written in `readings.md` with its time and its reason, and never the round's decision |
| Ad creatives and their optimization | The two rows above as two units; a round's `creatives` names the creatives it changed | As each row | As each row | As each row; what a round decided of a creative is read by the next creative's job from the rounds that name it |
