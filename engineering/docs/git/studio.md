# The studio: a repository that keeps producing

A studio is the repository in which a team and its agents keep turning out one kind of thing, or keep running one kind of round, day after day: card news for an account, ad creatives every week, the episodes of a series, the rounds in which running ads are tuned or a market is measured again; or several kinds, where one works on another. It is three things together: the layout every repository of this pack has ([layout.md](layout.md), [agreement.md](agreement.md), [console.md](console.md), [testing.md](testing.md)), laid by `git-repo-setup`; a small pack of its own, in which the studio's skills extend the pack's and what the studio knows is kept as pages an agent reads little of ([studio-know-how.md](studio-know-how.md)); and this page: the units and their states, the ledger, the screen a person reviews on, the commands and the check. Who works in a studio and how is [studio-operating.md](studio-operating.md). Read when a studio is set up, and before a skill adds a kind of file to one.

## What a studio adds to the tree

```
SKILLCDN.md             the studio's manifest: its name, its start page, the rules of every job here
studio.json             the units: each kind's folder, states, person states and ledger
skills/<job>/SKILL.md   one skill per job done here, each extending a skill of the pack
docs/start.md           the page every session reads first: which job is which skill
docs/policy.md          the person's standing answers, edited in place
docs/guides/<step>.md   what the studio knows about one step of the work, as it holds now
docs/trials.md          techniques tried: against what, with what result, what was decided
formats/<name>-v<n>/    the design as files; a change that alters finished work is a new folder
<folder>/_template/     the folder a new unit starts as
<folder>/<slug>/        one unit in progress: its record, inputs, outputs and review record
<folder>/<filed>/<day>/<slug>/    the same folder once it reached the last state
<ledger>                one row per unit of a kind: what was made or tried
<standing file>         what every round of a kind reads and adds to (a watch list)
scripts/                new, file, gallery, serve, check, and studio.mjs, which they share
gallery/                generated by the gallery command, never committed
.cache/                 what a run fetches or installs for itself, never committed
```

`docs/guides/`, `docs/trials.md` and `formats/` appear with their first content, as layout.md has it for every file. A folder under a unit's folder whose name starts with `_` is no unit (the template, examples). No page holds the studio's current state: the records hold it and the check prints it. The state flow and the commands are described for people in `docs/architecture.md` alone.

## The kinds of unit

A unit is one thing made or one round of ongoing work, and its folder, its record and its states have the same shape whichever it is.

| Kind | A unit is | Its folder holds | Its ledger row says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Something made | One post, one creative, one episode, one issue | What it is made from, what was rendered, the reviewer's record | What it is about, so that nothing is made twice |
| A round that changes | One change to something that runs, read after a set time: a budget moved, a creative swapped | What was changed and why, the time it is read, the readings, what was decided | What was tried on what, so that nothing is tried twice blind |
| A round that reads | One reading of the same measures by the same yardstick: the markets on a watch list, measured again | What that round read and what moved, as a report | Which scope and period, so that no period is read twice |

A round that reads works on a **standing file**, the one record every round of the kind reads and adds to (a watch list with its observations). It lies outside the units, at a path the kind's entry names, since no round owns it; a round's folder holds only its report and what it fetched. The method and the file's shape are the pack skill's that the studio extends; the studio keeps the file, the ledger as the log of the rounds, and the next round's date: the round that ends starts the next unit with the time it is due.

Most studios hold one kind. A studio holds several where one works on another: creatives and the rounds that tune the running ones are one repository, since a round names the creatives it changes and the next creative is written from what the rounds found; so are a magazine's issues and the rounds whose readings they print. Each kind has its own entry in `studio.json`, with its folder, template, states and ledger, and they meet in one field: a record names the units of another kind it is about by slug, in a field called as that kind's folder (a round's `creatives`, an issue's `rounds`). The review screen links the two both ways, and the check fails on a slug that names nothing. A second kind costs a session nothing it does not load: the start page routes by job, and a job reads another kind only through the slugs its unit names. Kinds that never name each other are separate studios.

## studio.json

`units` is a list with one entry per kind, a list of one for most studios; `limits` stands beside it.

| Field of a unit | Holds |
|---|---|
| `unit`, `folder` | The unit's name in one word (`post`, `creative`, `round`) and its folder (`posts`). The record file is `<unit>.json`. |
| `states` | The states in order, the first being the one a new unit starts in. `archived`, for a unit that will not go on, is always there, is not listed, and is set on the person's word. |
| `person` | The states only a person gives (an approval, a decision). A record at or past one holds `<state>: { "at", "by" }`, written by whoever received the person's answer, with their words. |
| `filed` | The last state. A unit in it is moved to `<folder>/<filed>/<day>/<slug>/`, the day being that of `<filed>.at` in its record. |
| `ledger` | The ledger's path, each kind's its own. |
| `standing` | The standing files of the kind, where it has any (`market/watch.json`). |
| `needs` | Per state, the files a unit's folder must hold from that state on. |
| `previews` | Paths inside a unit's folder, with `*` in the file name, of the pictures or video the review screen shows (`out/*.png`, `out/*.mp4`); the first that matches anything is used. Left out for a round. |
| `shows` | The record's fields the review screen prints beside the title: a round's change, its readings in a line, its decision. |
| `large` | The subfolders of a unit whose files go to large-file storage (`img`, `out`). |

`limits` holds the bounds the check keeps, in bytes, which is what a page costs a reader in any language, named only where a default does not fit: `AGENTS.md` 12,288, `docs/start.md` 4,096, `SKILLCDN.md` 3,072, `docs/policy.md` 8,192, `skill` (each studio skill) 8,192, `page` (each page of `docs/`) 16,384.

## A unit's record

`<unit>.json` holds `slug` (the folder's name, `YYYY-MM-DD-words-in-kebab-case`, the day being the one the unit is meant for), `title`, `status`, `owner` (one worker at a time), `task` (the board task), `created_at`, `format` (a folder under `formats/`, where the unit has a design), `notes`, and what the states add: the object of each person state, the object of the filed state with its `at`, and `<state>_for` for a state that waits for a time (`scheduled_for`; `running_for`, when a round is read; `planned_for`, when one is due), written with its zone. Where the studio holds several kinds, the field called as another kind's folder holds the slugs this unit is about.

A unit moves forward one state at a time, each written by whoever did the work of that state. A unit changed after a person saw it goes back to the state before theirs. Only the person's word sets a person state: a run that may go ahead alone still stops there. A person may give a state in advance for a class of units (every unit of the morning's look): the policy's Approval line then holds their words and the class, and the record's `by` names that line.

## The ledger

A Markdown table that ends its file, one per kind, one row per unit from the moment it is started: `| slug | state | what | keys | who | note |`. `what` is the unit in one line. `keys` are two to four specific names of what is made or tried (a person, an organization, a thing, an act; a round's object and change, or its scope and period), never a word as broad as the studio's field. `who` is the subject. A new unit that holds a key of another row is refused, and made all the same only on the person's word, which the row's `note` then carries. The state cell is never written by hand: the file command rewrites it from the record.

The ledger is the index of everything finished, and it only grows. An agent that asks whether something was made or tried searches it for a name, or lets the new command answer; it reads neither the whole table nor the filed folders. Where units leave the repository (posts on an account), the ledger is held against that place when a unit is filed, and what is out there without a row is added on the person's word.

## The review screen

The gallery command writes `gallery/index.html` from the records, and the serve command shows it at a local address, built again on every visit. Each kind has its section, with its standing files linked: first the units that wait for a person (those one state before a person state), then every other open unit by state, then the filed ones by day. A card shows the first preview (a video by its first frame), the title, the state, the owner, the task, the fields `shows` names and the slugs of the other kinds the unit names. A unit's own page shows every preview, a video with its controls, the units it names and those that name it as links, each Markdown record of its folder (`review.md`, one line per look: what was looked at, what was found or fixed; a round's `readings.md` or `report.md`) and the record whole.

The screen is for whoever sits at a checkout: a local address reaches nobody else, so a person elsewhere is asked on the board with the previews handed in. Their answer is written into the record by whoever received it. The screen writes nothing.

## The commands

Small scripts without dependencies, run from the root (`npm run <name>`), shipped as text by `git-studio-setup` and the studio's own once copied. `scripts/studio.mjs` holds what they share.

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `new [<unit>] <slug> --title --what --keys [--who --task --format --for --allowed]` | Copies `_template` to the unit's folder, fills the record with the first state, the owner and the day, and adds the ledger row. The unit's name comes first where the studio holds several kinds, and an option called as another kind's folder (`--creatives="<slug>, <slug>"`) records what the unit is about. `--for` writes the time the first state waits for (`planned_for`): the next round's date. Refuses a slug that exists and a key another row holds, unless `--allowed` carries the person's words. |
| `file` | Run after any change of state. Rewrites the ledgers' state cells from the records, and moves each unit in its filed state to its day's folder with `git mv`, rewriting the paths in its JSON files that climbed to the root. The moves are staged: one commit takes them with the records and the ledgers. |
| `gallery`, `serve` | The review screen, and the local address that shows it and every file of the repository, nothing cached. |
| `check` | Below. |

What makes the thing (a renderer, a measuring script, a sync of readings) is the making skill's, added under `scripts/` with the first unit and then the studio's own. No script of a studio sends anything out: that is a job's skill, after the person's state.

## The check

One command, the same before a commit and in continuous integration, where the checkout fetches no large file: a pointer is enough.

- **The configuration and the records:** each kind's name, folder and ledger its own, its standing files there; the slug's shape, the record's `slug` and `status`, the files `needs` names from each state on, the person's `at` and `by` from a person state on, the format's folder, and a unit for every slug a record names of another kind.
- **The ledger:** a row for every unit, its state equal to the record's, its `what` and `keys` filled from the first person state on, no key held twice without a note.
- **Storage:** every file under a `large` subfolder is tracked by git LFS.
- **The pages:** every relative link resolves; every page stays within its bound of `limits`; every page of `docs/` is linked from another; the manifest's `start` names a page that is there, the agreement names that page, and the page names every folder under `skills/` and every page of `docs/guides/`.
- **Warnings**, which a session deals with before anything else: a unit in the filed state that is not filed, and one whose `<state>_for` has passed while it still stands in that state. Until `AGENTS.md`, the start page and `docs/README.md` are written, a warning says that the routes and the orphan pages are not checked; from then on they fail the check.
- **Where the studio stands**, its last lines: each kind's open units by state, the slugs that wait for a person, the times that come next. A session starts from these.
- **A kind's own rules** are the default export of `scripts/check-<unit>.mjs`, called with that kind's units where the file exists: a caption's length, a slide's fact, at most one round running on one market. A rule that runs kept breaking moves here from its sentence.

A second job of continuous integration runs the indexer's check on the studio as a pack and looks up the pack skills it extends (studio-know-how.md "The checks").

## Starting values by kind

The plan starts from the row that fits and changes it with the user; a request that names several kinds takes each row as a unit of one studio. Only the first row is the shape of a studio that ran for weeks; the others are derived from the pack's skills.

| Studio | `unit`, `folder` | `states`, the person's in bold | Keys name | The pack skills its skills extend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A card news account | `post`, `posts` | idea, brief, draft, review, **approved**, scheduled, published | The event: its people, organizations, things, acts | `web-card-news-topic-brief`, `html-card-news-from-brief`, `html-card-news-batch`, `instagram-post-scheduling`. The paths, the policy's headings and the states are those of the card news set's [account record](/docs/card-news/account.md), followed as written |
| Ad creatives | `creative`, `creatives` | idea, script, draft, review, **approved**, launched | The product, the hook, the reference it started from | `meta-ad-library-creative-research`, `higgsfield-shorts-ad`, `meta-ads-setup` for the launch |
| A series of short videos | `episode`, `episodes` | premise, script, draft, review, **approved**, published | The premise, the characters | `higgsfield-shorts-drama` |
| Ad optimization | `round`, `rounds` | planned, running, read, **decided** | The object (a market, a campaign, an ad set) and the change | `meta-ads-setup`, its monitoring phase with [monitoring.md](/engineering/docs/meta-marketing-api/monitoring.md): a round's change is one a rule there proposes, `running_for` the end of that rule's evidence window, `readings.md` the site's figures beside the platform's for the window, and the decision (keep, revert, scale, pause) the person's. What the policy lets an agent do in a running round without asking (a pause at a bound it states) is written in `readings.md` with its time and reason, and is never the round's decision |
| Ad creatives and their optimization | The two rows above as two units; a round's `creatives` names the creatives it changed | As each row | As each row | As each row; the next creative's job reads what the rounds that name a creative decided |
| A market watch | `round`, `rounds`; `standing` the watch list (`market/watch.json`, its ad snapshots beside it) | planned, read, **reviewed** | The scope and the period | `web-market-entry-signals`, its later round, with `meta-ad-library-rising-products` counting the same topics again. `report.md` holds what moved; what that skill leaves to the person (a theme raised to testing, a dropped one reopened) is given with `reviewed`; the round that ends starts the next with `--for`. A magazine made from the watch (`html-signal-magazine`) is a second unit, `issue`, whose `rounds` names the rounds it prints |
