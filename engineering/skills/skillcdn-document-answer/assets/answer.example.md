# The shape of an answer

Sent as the delivery, with the quotes in the pack's language. Angle brackets are placeholders; a line in angle brackets is left out when its case did not arise.

```markdown
**<The answer, in two or three sentences, in the shape the question asked for.>**

<Where the question's term is not the pack's:> Read '<the user's term>' as the pack's '<term>': "<the quote that equates them>" — <path>, <heading path>, offset <n> limit <m>. <Or:> The pack does not use '<the user's term>'; what it says about '<the term its maps and sections use for the same thing>' follows.

Sources (read from commit <commit>):
- "<the quote, word for word>" — <path>, <Heading> > <Subheading>, offset <n> limit <m>
- "<the quote>" — <path>, <heading path>, offset <n> limit <m><; read for the earlier question, where it was><; commit <commit>, where it differs from the one above>

<Where two documents differ:> <path A> says "<quote>" and <path B> says "<quote>"; the pack does not say which holds.

<Where one section alone answers:> No other section read says the same.

<Where the check dropped a quote:> One quote did not hold on the second read and was dropped.

Not stated by the pack: <what the question wanted and no section states>. The nearest passage: "<quote>" — <path>, <heading path>, offset <n> limit <m>. <Where to look instead, when the pack names it.>

<What to do next, where the pack names it.>

<When the user wants it kept:> To keep this answer in the pack as a note for the next time, skillcdn-document-research writes it.
```

The heading path is written as the result gives it, with ">" between its levels. The "Not stated by the pack" line is left out when every part of the question was answered. A question the pack does not cover at all keeps the first line ("Not stated by the pack."), the nearest passage, read and checked like any citation, and what was tried (the three searches and the outline, in the pack's words).
