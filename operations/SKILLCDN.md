# Rules for operations skills

These add to the rules of the repository.

**Nothing internal crosses.** A document that goes to a client or a partner carries nothing the organization said for its own ears (its costs, staffing, judgments, doubts, terms it has not agreed to); what stays out is listed before the document is written and recorded where the organization keeps its own notes.

**The records come first.** The facts of a meeting, a visit or a delivery (who, when, where, what was agreed before) are read from the organization's records where they exist, never asked for first and never invented; what a run learns is written back there.
