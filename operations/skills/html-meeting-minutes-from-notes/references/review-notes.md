# Review notes: what minutes are sent back for

The composition rules in SKILL.md came out of review rounds on real minutes. This page keeps the notes a reviewer gives, in their general form, with the move that answers each, so that the next agent recognizes the note on its own draft and makes the move before the reviewer has to. Read it when a draft comes back with notes, and before writing minutes for a reviewer who has sent some back before. Nothing here names a client, a project or a person; a note that names one item usually names a default the whole document repeats, and the move fixes the default.

## The top and the header

| The note | The move |
|---|---|
| The titles are colored and a colored bar stands beside each section; it reads as machine-made. | Grayscale only: black type, gray rules, a light gray header cell. A section title is bold text over a thin rule. |
| The logos are too big, and the organization's comes before the client's. | Each logo a line of text's height, the client's first, a hairline between them, on the top rule; the document kind at the right in small gray type. |
| A subtitle under the title and a distribution row in the header add nothing. | Both removed. The header table has four rows: date and time, place, attendees, author. |
| The author row carries a date, and the overview dates the slides that were shown. | Dates that carry no information are cut; the meeting's date is in the header already. |
| The attendee list names people the notes mention but the records do not confirm. | The confirmed names with their titles, then "and others" in the minutes' language; a name is never invented and a title never guessed. The place is its official name, from the records. |

## The sections

| The note | The move |
|---|---|
| The items are labels with fragments after them. | Numbered items, each a bold lead and one plain sentence; a table only for the actions and for figures given in the room. |
| "Pending" and "under discussion" do not say what the reader does with the item. | The section is "items to confirm" (확인사항): what someone checks before the next meeting. |
| The actions table says "due", which reads as a deadline nobody set. | "Planned" (예정): a date, "the next meeting", "on delivery". |
| The actions list what the other party does on its own. | Only what both sides wait on, or what the organization does; the other party's own errands are not minuted. |
| A table note is a full sentence with a period, and reads as the minutes' own opinion. | A note ends in a noun or a verb stem ("the vendor's quote to be confirmed"; in Korean the noun form, 확인이 필요). |
| The overview retells how the meeting went. | Two sentences at most: what the meeting went through, by its agenda. |

## The wording

| The note | The move |
|---|---|
| A decision is hedged. | A decision is written as what will be done, in one sentence, with the actor; a hedge belongs to an answer, not to a decision. |
| An answer the organization gave is firmer than what was said. | What was said and no more: "is expected to", not "will"; a figure quoted in the room carries its caveat in the same sentence. |
| The organization's own tool is named in the minutes. | The generic noun for the kind of thing it is ("the framework it is built on", "the mail service"); the product's name stays in the organization's own record, and "the tool" is not a noun either. |
| The ownership of what is delivered is vague where it was decided. | Firm and short: who owns what is delivered, in one sentence. |
| The minutes carry the organization's own judgments and its reasons for a figure. | Nothing said for the room's own ears: it goes into the organization's own record, and the split checkpoint lists it. |
| A middle dot, a dash and a circled number crept in. | The house-style check is the gate; the file is fixed and rendered again. |

## What generalizes

- A note that names one item usually names a default the whole document repeats. Fix the default, then every item.
- The reader is the person on the client's side who has to act on the minutes the day after the meeting: every sentence is something they can act on or check.
- A visual check is a check only when it is written down: a row cut across pages, a heading alone at a page's foot and a logo too large are on the phase 4 list because each reached a reviewer.
- Facts the reviewer confirms (a title, a place's name, a date) are recorded where the organization keeps its records, and the minutes file is the only thing edited.
