# Reading posts for the spec

Which posts the study reads, how each slide is measured and recorded, and what is taken from the profile and the caption. How the site is read without signing in, and what a post page carries, are in the repository's Instagram set, [signed-out.md](/docs/instagram/signed-out.md) and [posts.md](/docs/instagram/posts.md), both included with this skill.

## The profile

Set the viewport first and open the profile as signed-out.md says ("The viewport", "What is visible"), closing the prompt by its own control ("The sign-in prompt"). Each visible post is opened once, in phase 2, one at a time with a few seconds between loads; on opening, read its post object first (posts.md "The post object"): the time, the slide count and each slide's media type, the caption whole. Write into `profile.md` a table of id, date and time in the account's market's time zone (the market read from the bio and the captions' language; where it cannot be read, UTC, said in the delivery), kind, slide count and the caption's first line; the kind is settled once the slides are read. The bio, the name line and the link in bio say what the account calls itself and who runs it; record them as the account's own words, leaving out a personal contact such as an e-mail address.

## Which posts to read

The spec needs every slide type the account uses, and a type is only known from the slides. Read, slide by slide, at least one post of each kind the account has among the visible posts:

| Kind | How to recognize it | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| A story about a person or an issue | A photo of someone or something on the cover, a two-line headline, several slides of text | The story slide's anatomy and density, the note and collage variants, whether the last slide carries a call to action |
| An information or visit post | A place, a product or an event; prices, dates or a location somewhere | Boxed captions, tags, an information slide at the end |
| A list or weekly post | A number in the headline, a series label | The lead slide: a two-line sub, a small body, a source tag |
| An event or sponsored post | A sponsor's name as the label, a date range | The poster slide: large type, a date, little else |
| A report on broadcast or captured material | Frames of a broadcast, or screenshots of a post, stacked whole with their logos, labels and subtitles kept; no text of the account's after the cover | Whether the account keeps the material's marks and captions and adds nothing of its own; the margins around the frames; how many frames a slide holds and how they are stacked |
| A humor or meme post | A joke in the headline, a reaction image, a screenshot of a post with a line of the account's | What carries the joke, whether the account writes over the material, how short it stays |
| A single image | No dots under the post | What the account does when there is one slide |
| A video | A play badge | Whether video slides mix into carousels |

Read every post visible without signing in (about a dozen at the time of writing): each carousel slide by slide, a single image as a one-slide post, a video at three frames. A type seen once stays tentative, what the account never does on a kind is only said of a kind read twice, and a spec written from half the visible posts missed the build the account used most, which the user noticed when they asked whether every post had been read. Only when the user limits the reading is the floor three kinds and two posts of the kind the user's own post will be (the account's most common kind when the request names none). A post the user names is read whatever its kind. A kind the table lacks gets a key of its own in `kinds`, named in plain words, and the delivery names it. One post named as the reference is read first and its kind is the user's; the account's other visible posts of that kind are read to confirm its types. For a genre, one carousel of the user's kind on each account, a type confirmed where two accounts use it.

An account often builds one kind several ways: a story as a long feature of sentences over photos, as a photo story carried by short boxed lines, or as a short news post of a cover and one or two photos with the facts in the caption. Name each build by its slide count and what carries the text, record the builds as `kinds` in the spec, and apply "two of the user's kind" to the build when the posts allow it. A video post is read at three frames for its text and layout (posts.md "A video post"); its motion is not part of the card format.

## Stepping and reading

Step each carousel and read every slide at zoom as posts.md "Stepping a carousel" and "Reading a slide" say, the whole carousel to its last slide; where no control steps it, stop and say so, because the slides' text is only in the images. Each slide's row is recorded before stepping on.

## Measuring sizes and colors

Text is counted; everything else on a slide is estimated and marked so in the row and in the spec. The post's image box on the screenshot is the canvas: a size is the element's height as a fraction of the box's height, converted to the canvas's documented pixel height (a line of body text that is 2.3% of the box is about 31 pixels on a 1350-pixel canvas); a line height is the distance between two baselines over the font size; a weight is a class read at zoom (regular 400, bold 700, heavy 800); a color is the nearest hex read by eye, with a saturated label color named by hue; a gradient's darkness is the fraction of black where the type sits; a position is each block's edges as percentages of the box, its top and bottom always and its left and right where it does not span the margins, read from the pixel rows and columns where the block starts and ends on the screenshot. "Centered" or "bottom" alone is not a position, because the build places every block by these edges, and an edge read by eye from where a block seems to sit was off by a sixth of the canvas once. The example JSON carries `estimate` beside these fields. A font is named by its letterforms as the closest free equivalent, with `guess` true. A supplied image (a screenshot or a saved slide the user sent, a sample a search found) is measured the same way: shown one slide at a time, at the canvas width, in a page of the run's own in the browser at hand, driven or headless, so that the screenshot, the zoom and the pixel rows are read as on a post page; on a phone screenshot the post's box is the region between the post's top and bottom edges, found once per post, and the chrome around it is not the canvas.

## Samples from a search

Where the site cannot be reached and no browser of the user's, no page of the account's own and no slides of the user's read the reference (SKILL.md Inputs, "Samples"), the genre is read from samples found with the tools at hand. Two searches, both in the market's language: a web search for the genre's words with "card news" and the platform's name, and for a named reference its account's name with "card news", which returns the pages that carry card news with the account's name on them (an outlet's, a party's or an organization's own site, a forum's or a blog's repost of a post with the account's mark on the slides) and names the genre's accounts; and an image search engine's results page opened in the browser at hand, driven or headless, never fetched by a plain request, which gets another, thinner page, and never by one dressed up as a browser's: the page lists card news images each with the address of the page it sits on, carried in each result's data beside the image's address (one engine, checked 2026-10), and a headless browser screenshots the page and reads those addresses from it. The query is the genre's words with "card news" and the platform's name, without the story's words: that query found dozens on one results page, the genre's word with "card news" alone found unrelated pictures, and queries that carried the story's words found none (checked 2026-10). Open the page a result sits on and read the image there, never the thumbnail: the page shows the account (its name in the text, or its mark on the slides) and the image at full size, often the whole carousel with the post's date and caption; a community forum's or a blog's repost whose slides carry the account's mark, and a party's or an outlet's own page, are such pages and are most of what a results page lists; a page that shows no account, or only a thumbnail, is skipped and another found. Show each slide at the canvas width in a page of the run's own (an `img` of the image's address inside a box of the canvas size, so that no file is written), screenshot it, and measure as above; an image narrower than about half the canvas gives its anatomy and its counts, and its sizes and colors are marked as estimates from a small image. One carousel per account where the page carries one, else the cover and the body slides it shows; three to five accounts, a type counted where two show it. Each slide is a row below, the page as the post's id, and `profile.md` lists the search, each page, the account and the day. The samples are a sample of the genre and not of one account: nothing is said of what an account never does, and a type, a density or a register no sample showed is left out of the spec and named, for the variations to design.

## What to record

One row per slide, before stepping on:

| Column | What goes in |
|---|---|
| `post`, `kind`, `slide`, `of` | The post id, its kind, the slide's number and the slide count. |
| `photo` | Full-bleed photo cropped to the canvas; a frame of a broadcast or a screenshot of a post kept whole at its own ratio, with the marks it keeps (a logo, a label, subtitles) and its top and bottom edges as percentages of the box, each frame's own when two are stacked, so that the margins above, between and below follow; two photos or frames stacked or side by side; video; flat color, as hex; the gradient (bottom, top, none) and how dark; a dim overlay. Whether the text on the slide is the account's own or the material's; the material's text is named (subtitles, a label, a logo) with where it sits, and not counted. |
| `label` | The small label above the headline or sub: its words' kind (a series name, a sponsor), color, weight, italic, position. |
| `headline`, `sub` | Lines, characters per line, size (estimate), weight, color, position; whether it is a news title, a question, a fragment, a noun phrase, a quote. |
| `body` | Sentences, characters per sentence, lines, size and line height (estimates), weight, color, position; the ending of each sentence (declarative, a sentence-final noun phrase, a quote); one fact per sentence or not. |
| `note` | A short serif or script line on a photo-only slide: lines, characters, size (estimate), position, face. |
| `tags`, `boxes` | A source tag in a corner; a boxed caption with its colors; a price or location tag. |
| `other` | Anything this slide does that the others do not. |

Transcribe the account's own text from the zoomed image as it is read and count its characters in its script, spaces included, with Node.js or by hand; a count is the row, and the transcription is working material that stays out of the log. A sentence ends at a sentence-final ending or at a line break the account uses as one. A slide with no text is a row with `photo` and `note` empty or not, and that absence is a measurement too. The rows go into `slide-log.md` as one table; the log may hold a slide's ending or first words where they are needed to count or to classify, and it stays in the user's folder.

Some elements ride on a slide of another type rather than being a slide of their own: a serif note on a story slide, an inset (a product's package, a poster, an archive photo placed as a rectangle on the photo), a talk box (a white box of one or two short lines), a source card (a screenshot of the original post), a source tag. Record them in `other` and in the spec's `elements`, each with the slide types it rides on.

A slide log from an earlier, interrupted run of the same request is kept: check its rows against the post objects (dates, slide counts, media types), re-split its columns into the ones above without changing a measurement, and read on from the first post it lacks.

## The caption

Read the caption whole (posts.md "The caption"). Record its parts in order (facts with a date and an outlet, context, a closing line, mentions, a credit line), its endings against the slides' endings, the exact pattern of its credit line, and any phrase the voice repeats across captions. The caption's words are data about the account's register, described in the spec as patterns; a first line may be quoted in `profile.md` and in `categories` to identify the post, and nothing of the caption is copied beyond that.
