# Hand-off to a designer: the note of an issue

The shape of `handoff.md` in an issue's folder, with placeholder values. It goes with the PDF and the one-file HTML to the designer who refines the issue; the user confirms it before it is put anywhere.

## The file to work from

`<magazine>-<YYYY-MM>.pdf` and `.html`, the edition rendered on `<day>`. This edition is the base: an earlier one is not.

## What to keep

- The ground and its one light, as on the brand's site; the cards as the site draws them.
- One accent, on about one figure a slide. The point colour on single dots only.
- The serif in the masthead and the foot mark alone; the mono for page numbers, the period and figures on a scale.
- The small figures beside the numbers on the cover and the contents: one shape an item, drawn from the values.
- The signal marks of the verdicts: three bars, two bars, one dashed bar.
- A company card's parts: the logo, the name, one line, two figures.
- Every number and every sentence. A wording that should change comes back as a comment, not as an edit.
