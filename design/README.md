Skills for design work: slides and decks, card news posts and the formats they are built in, brand assets, icons and store graphics, made in the team's design tool, drafted as HTML and PDF before the tool, or varied as looks compared on a contact sheet. The area manifest next to this file ([SKILLCDN.md](SKILLCDN.md)) says who these skills are for and adds the rules every design skill follows on top of the repository's. The area mounts alone at `skillcdn.ai/gh/skillcdn/skills/design` and installs as the `design` plugin of the repository's Claude Code marketplace.

| Skill | Tool family | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [`skills/figma-slides-from-content/`](skills/figma-slides-from-content/) | Figma | A document (a proposal, a report, any PDF) moved into an existing Figma Slides deck as new slides, one per page, in the deck's own design system. Read the document's text with its sizes, positions and breaks and its images, measure the existing slides for the type scale, colors, margins, title treatment, bullet marks, cards and shadows, plan one slide per page with a layout type, build through the Figma MCP server, and audit every slide by numbers and zoomed renders: alignment on the glyphs, one spec per element role, nothing outside a card's padding, no word broken mid-way. Existing and user-edited slides stay untouched. |
| [`skills/html-deck-from-outline/`](skills/html-deck-from-outline/) | Browser | A first-draft slide deck (a kickoff presentation, a proposal, a status report) as one self-contained HTML file and a PDF from a content outline. Turn the outline into a deck file in which every slide is a type and a list of blocks (a statement beside a definition list, numbered rows, a loop, a Gantt-style schedule, a system diagram with a process strip, brand panels with real logos, team profiles), render it at a fixed 16:9 page size in the organization's own tokens, print the PDF with a Chromium-family browser, check the wording against the house style the deck declares, and review a contact sheet of every page before delivery; structure from type, rules and real marks, with no icon card grids. A designer then polishes a finished structure. Nothing is spent. |
| [`skills/html-design-variations/`](skills/html-design-variations/) | Browser | Four looks for one design (a page's first screen, a deck's cover with a body slide, a post's cover, a card news format's cover and body slide, a frame) as HTML rendered in a browser and tiled on one contact sheet, from a reference, a concept or the thing as it is. Read the reference's aesthetics for what the look must say, place candidates on the design axes near or far as the request asks, render each as its representative screens, judge each for the AI feel before it is shown, compare them with a recommendation and a runner-up, and hand the pick's tokens to the skill that produces, a card news format as a spec the build follows. Nothing is spent. |
| [`skills/instagram-card-news-format-study/`](skills/instagram-card-news-format-study/) | Instagram | A card-news format as a spec, from a reference account, one reference post or a few accounts of a genre, read without signing in: the canvas and slide count, each slide type's anatomy and how it treats its photo (cropped, or a broadcast frame kept whole with its marks), which text is the account's own, the text density counted on the slides, the register of slides and of captions as patterns, the caption's parts and credit line, which variant carries which content, what the account never does, and the nearest free fonts and colors; every post visible without signing in is read; where the site refuses the host, the account's own site, supplied slides or search samples stand in, said so. The account's photos and words never enter a result. |
| [`skills/html-card-news-from-brief/`](skills/html-card-news-from-brief/) | Browser | The carousel from a brief in a format spec's format (measured from a reference, or designed from one at the user's pick; with none, the routes to one offered), as HTML rendered to PNG by a headless browser: every slide one of the spec's slide types, chosen by what it carries and sequenced as the spec's build runs, no layouts of the skill's own; one thread from the published angle at the format's density and register; the brief's images placed as each type treats them and credited; previewed, every PNG reviewed at native and phone size, delivered with the caption, the credits and a pre-publish checklist. |

The card news skills chain. `html-card-news-from-brief` takes its format from `instagram-card-news-format-study`, as measured or as `html-design-variations` varied it at the user's pick, and its facts and images from the product area's `web-card-news-topic-brief`; with no spec it offers the four routes to one, with no brief the research, and runs what the user chooses before its plan; the study, the variations and the research stay general.

Documents for this area go in `docs/` next to this file, where SkillCDN discovers them by default; there are none yet, and the Figma skill keeps what it knows about its tool in its own `references/` until a second Figma skill shares it. The card news skills read the four routes to a format spec and the shape of the spec and the brief they pass between them from the repository's [`docs/card-news/`](../docs/card-news/README.md), shared with the research skill in `product/`; the deck and the card news skills read what every browser skill shares from [`docs/browser/`](../docs/browser/README.md), the variations what every variation skill shares from [`docs/variation/`](../docs/variation/README.md), and the study how Instagram is read without signing in from [`docs/instagram/`](../docs/instagram/README.md).

How to write a skill: [guide/skill-authoring.md](../guide/skill-authoring.md). How to add one here: [guide/adding.md](../guide/adding.md). The `node scripts/check.mjs` check fails when a skill directory is missing from this table.
