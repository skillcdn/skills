# Handoff: what the pick carries to the skill that makes it

A variation skill ends with the user's pick and does not make the thing. The pick is handed to the skill that produces in the medium, in that skill's own terms, so that its intake takes it without translation; the user starts that skill with the pick. The handoff is one page in the run's folder: the effect brief, the pick's row of the comparison sheet, the mechanism line, what must stay, the sample, and the fixes the verdict made, followed by the producing skill's inputs filled in as far as the pick fills them.

## What every handoff carries

| Item | Why the producing skill needs it |
|---|---|
| The effect brief | Its review holds the result against the readings, not against the reference's surface. |
| The pick's position on the axes, in the producing skill's vocabulary | Its plan starts from positions, not from the reference. |
| The mechanism line and what must stay | The one thing it may not change while it makes everything else. |
| The sample (the opening lines; the representative screen) | The checkpoint where the user said yes; what the first draft is checked against. |
| The verdict's fixes | What was already removed, so that the producing skill's own review does not put it back. |
| The other references read, by where they were seen | For the producing skill's own research step, where it has one; never reused. |
| The assumptions the run rests on (which piece was read, the market, the medium) and what was left out | So that the producing skill, and its user, check them before building. |

## By producing skill

| Pick | Producing skill | What it takes, where | Seam |
|---|---|---|---|
| A concept for a card news post: the angle's reading, the thread's order, the register's level | `html-card-news-from-brief` (marketing), after `web-card-news-topic-brief` has written the brief | The pick names the angle among the brief's published readings and the order of beats; the build's plan checkpoint takes them as its thesis and thread, within the spec's build. The register's level is the spec's: a pick that moves it is not for this account. | Untried: no run has handed a concept to the build. |
| A look for a card news post | none | The look of a card news post is the reference account's measured format, and a variation of it is a different account: the design skill says so and offers a concept variation instead. | Settled by the format's rule. |
| A concept for a deck: its line, the order of slides, what the room already knows | `html-deck-from-outline` (design) | The pick rewrites the outline's order and the one sentence per slide; the deck skill's phase 1 reads the outline as it is. | Untried. |
| A look for a deck: palette, type, composition | `html-deck-from-outline` (design) | The palette as `meta.theme` tokens, the type as the font and its scale, the composition as the choice among the deck's blocks; the design floor still holds. | Untried. |
| A look for an app or a site: palette, type, shape, tone, symbol | `cloudflare-new-app` (engineering) | The pick fills the look section of the brief the skill writes (`docs/brand.md`: palette, type, shape and motion, tone, symbol), with its reasons; the skill's own design checkpoint shows it. | Untried. |
| A concept for an ad: stake, figure, device, form, order of telling | `higgsfield-shorts-ad` (marketing) | The pick is the story's rule, device, figure and hook before the skill writes five hooks of its own; its hook table and thumb test still gate the kept hook. | Untried: the ad skill was not changed for this set, and its first run with a handoff waits for a run that can be made. |
| A premise for a drama series | `higgsfield-shorts-drama` (media) | The pick is the premise and the rule of the world the bible is written from. | Untried, as above. |
| A concept for a video that is not an ad (a launch film, an explainer) or for any medium the pack has no skill for | none | The handoff page is the brief for whoever makes it, and the delivery says that no skill of the pack produces it. | Settled by the catalog. |
| A look for slides in an existing design file | `figma-slides-from-content` (design) | None: the look is the deck's own measured design system, and the skill does not vary it. | Settled by the skill's rule. |

A seam is marked tested when one request that needs both skills has run through to the producing skill's first checkpoint, and the producing skill's intake takes the handoff page without a question it should not have to ask; the test and its date go in the row.

## What a handoff never does

It never runs the producing skill, never estimates its cost, never pre-answers its questions beyond what the pick decides, and never carries the reference's own lines, screens or people. A producing skill that finds the handoff incomplete asks its own intake question, as it would without one.
