# Handoff: what the pick carries to the skill that makes it

A variation skill ends with the user's pick and does not make the thing. The pick is handed to the skill that produces in the medium, in that skill's own terms, so that its intake takes it without translation; the user starts that skill with the pick. The handoff is one page in the run's folder: the effect brief, the pick's row of the comparison sheet, the mechanism line, what must stay, the sample, and the fixes the verdict made, followed by the producing skill's inputs filled in as far as the pick fills them.

## What every handoff carries

| Item | Why the producing skill needs it |
|---|---|
| The effect brief | Its review holds the result against the readings, not against the reference's surface. |
| The pick's position on the axes, in the producing skill's vocabulary | Its plan starts from positions, not from the reference. |
| The mechanism line and what must stay | The one thing it may not change while it makes everything else. |
| The sample (the opening lines; the representative screen) | The checkpoint where the user said yes; what the first draft is checked against. |
| The verdict's fixes | What was already removed, so that the producing skill's own review does not put it back. |
| The other references read, by where they were seen | For the producing skill's own research step, where it has one; never reused. |
| The assumptions the run rests on (which piece was read, the market, the medium) and what was left out | So that the producing skill, and its user, check them before building. |

A seam is marked tested when one request that needs both skills has run through to the producing skill's first checkpoint, and the producing skill's intake takes the handoff page without a question it should not have to ask; the test and its date go in the row.

## A card news format

A card news post's look is its format: the slide types and their anatomy, the density, the register, the fonts and the colors, written as a format spec that the build follows slide by slide ([format-spec.md](/docs/card-news/format-spec.md)). A look for it is therefore a format of the user's own, made on one of the routes in format-spec.md "Where a spec comes from", and the design variations take it this way:

- **The start** is a format spec from `instagram-card-news-format-study`: the reference's, measured, for a slight change or for something new from it; the genre's common form for a format of the user's own. With no spec at hand, the study runs first, on the reference the request names or on the genre; a request for the whole post is better started at the card news build, which runs the study, the research and the variations in order. The start's positions are the candidates' first row, written in its fields: `colors`, `fonts`, the anatomy of `cover` and of the text type of the build the brief's story takes (else the spec's most common build), each anatomy's `photo`, `density`, `register`.
- **The crowd** is the genre's spec where the study read one. For something new from a reference, the study also reads the reference's genre in the same run, one post on each of three to five other accounts, into a genre spec beside the reference's, as it reads a genre, under its own rules for the site; where it cannot, the crowd is written from what the agent knows of the genre and marked unseen. A slight change needs no crowd, since no look goes far. Accounts are cited by handle in the effect brief, never on the sheet.
- **The unit** is the cover and one body slide of that build, at the spec's canvas, each look's two screens side by side on the sheet. Their words and photos are the topic brief's where a brief exists (its working headline in the start's headline style, one beat's facts written by the variations as sentences at the start's density, since the brief's beats name facts and the build writes the post's own sentences later, the cover image and the beat's image), else the request's, the same on every look.
- **The distance** follows the route, as [axes.md](/docs/variation/axes.md) "The near and the far candidate" says: a slight change holds the start's slide types and moves one or two axes; a format of the user's own and something new may also move the types' composition and the imagery's treatment, among the treatments of the brief's images (cropped full-bleed, kept whole with its marks, a page capture, flat color with none) and never to an illustration, a diagram or a still the brief does not hold, since the build places only the brief's images; and each look says in a line what it does to the types it does not show. The density and the register stay the start's (a concept variation moves a register), except the characters per line of a block whose width a look changed, counted on its render, and except where the request moves the density itself (less text, more): then density is an axis like the others, each look names the counts it sets (sentences per slide, lines, characters per line, what goes to the caption), and the designed spec carries the pick's counts as chosen values. A format measured on a genre is not lightened unasked; a user who finds the result too full asks, and the build or the variations move it.
- **The handoff** writes, in a folder of the format's own beside the brief folders (`card-news/<the format's name>/`, named for the user's account or magazine where they give one, else in a few words from the request, said at the sheet), `format-spec.json` as format-spec.md says of a designed spec, with `source` naming the start, the route, the axes moved and the look, and `style-notes.md` with the line the look must say, each type's look in a paragraph, and what the build's review holds against it. The build reads it next: in the run that offered the route; where the variations were asked for directly, the delivery names the build as the next step, with the research before it where there is no brief.

## What a handoff never does

It never runs the producing skill, never estimates its cost, never pre-answers its questions beyond what the pick decides, and never carries the reference's own lines, screens or people. A producing skill that finds the handoff incomplete asks its own intake question, as it would without one.
