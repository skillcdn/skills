# Variation: what its skills share

The skills that make variations of something (concept directions in product, looks in design) share one method: read the reference for what it does before what it looks like; find other references that get the same effect by other means; set the axes a variation can move on and place candidates on them, one far and one near; judge every candidate for the AI feel before the user sees it; compare them on one sheet with a recommendation and a runner-up; and hand the user's pick to the skill that makes the thing. The method lives here once, medium by medium where a medium differs, with no medium's tool, call or price in it: how a candidate is rendered, and what that costs, is the medium's own, in the pages of the family that renders it; a skill of the pack is named only where a handoff goes. Each variation skill includes the pages every run reads, links the rest, and carries its own workflow, checkpoints, verdicts and rules itself.

## Who reads this

- [`web-concept-variations`](/product/skills/web-concept-variations/SKILL.md) (product) includes `effect-brief.md`, `axes.md`, `not-ai.md` and `comparison.md`, and links `handoff.md` from its delivery. Its candidates are directions: a rule, a figure, a device, an order of telling, written as lines, never rendered.
- [`html-design-variations`](/design/skills/html-design-variations/SKILL.md) (design) includes the same four and the browser set's `rendering-html.md`, and links `handoff.md`. Its candidates are looks: a palette, type, composition, imagery, rendered as one representative screen each by the cheapest means of the medium; for a page or a slide that is HTML in a browser, by the repository's browser pages; for a medium whose stills are generated, by that family's pages, which say what a still costs and when the user is asked. The design skill itself spends nothing.
- The skills that produce keep their own lists where they are sharper than this set's (the card news register checks, the deck's design floor, the ad's hook table) and take a pick as `handoff.md` says; the card news build and the deck link `not-ai.md` from their review, and the ad and drama skills will when a run of them can be made.

The pages are reached by root-relative paths (`/docs/variation/<topic>.md`); an included page arrives with the skill wherever SkillCDN serves it, a linked one is read through the repository connection when its phase comes. A copy of a skill, a plugin install or a mount of one area lacks what is not included: each skill's Requirements say so and name this directory.

## What every variation skill does the same way

- **Effect before surface.** A reference is read for its intent, its effect, its aesthetics, its fun, its expected effect and the prior knowledge it rests on, and the mechanism is written in one line, before any candidate is thought of. A variation of the surface copies the surface; a variation of the effect finds new surfaces.
- **Other references, never the reference's surface.** Three to five other pieces that get the same effect by other means are found, by the effect and the device and not by the subject; what each does differently is where candidates come from. Nothing of the reference's words, people, marks or layout enters a candidate.
- **Candidates on axes, four to six, one near and one far.** Each moves on two or three axes and holds the rest, so that the sheet compares moves and not impressions; the near one moves on one axis and is the control, the far one moves where the reference's genre crowds and is the open angle.
- **The AI-feel verdict before anything is shown.** Every candidate passes the signals and the positive tests of `not-ai.md`, or is fixed, or is dropped; none is shown with a caveat.
- **One sheet, a recommendation, a runner-up, the user's pick.** The sheet reads positions and mechanisms; the recommendation is argued in the effect brief's terms; the runner-up says what it would give instead; the far candidate is kept on the sheet even when not recommended. The user picks, merges or asks for more on one axis, and a merge goes through the verdict again.
- **The cheapest render of the medium, or none.** A concept is shown as lines; a look as one representative screen made by the medium's cheapest means. What a render costs, and the consent it needs, is the medium's family's rule, applied there; a variation skill estimates nothing and spends nothing of its own.
- **The pick goes to the skill that makes the thing.** In that skill's own terms, as `handoff.md` says; the variation skill never runs it.

Each skill states these in its hard rules, in the form its workflow gives them, so that they hold where these pages do not travel.

## The common and the special

This set holds what is true of a variation in any medium. A signal, a test or a rule that is true of one medium only lives with that medium's skill, and this set names it as an example at most: the ad skill's hook table, written for a spoken hook in a short video, stays in the ad skill and is cited in `not-ai.md`; the card news build's register checks and the deck's design floor likewise. Where a producing skill's list says the same thing in sharper words, the sharper words win there. The ad and drama skills were not changed when this set was written, and their seams with it are marked untried in `handoff.md` until a run of them can be made.

## How these pages are written

Each page is organized by topic and says what holds now, in the imperative where it is a procedure and as a statement where it is a fact. Examples use stand-ins (a product, an account, a deck of a kind) and never a real reference, brand, person or client; a run's own subject, lines and candidates stay out, and what a run taught goes into the sentence it bears on. No medium's tool is named and no price is quoted, because a medium's family pages carry those; a skill of the pack is named where a handoff goes.
