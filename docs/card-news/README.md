# Card news: what its skills share

The skills that make card news (`instagram-card-news-format-study`, `html-card-news-from-brief` and `html-card-news-batch` in the design area, `web-card-news-topic-brief` in the product area, `html-design-variations` when the user wants a format of their own) and the one that posts it (`instagram-post-scheduling` in the marketing area) pass two artifacts between them: a **format spec**, measured from a reference or designed from one at the user's pick, and a **topic brief**, the sourced facts and the images of one story. An account that posts day after day also keeps a **record**: its policy, each post's state and a ledger of what it has covered. The shape of each lives here once, so that the skill that writes it and the skill that reads it agree without restating each other. Each skill carries its own method (how posts are read, how facts are sourced, how slides are composed and rendered, how a post is booked) and its own rules.

| Document | Holds | Read |
|---|---|---|
| [`format-spec.md`](format-spec.md) | The routes to a spec in the order they are offered (the reference varied, near or far; the reference as it is; with no reference, candidates to pick one from, and the genre's common form only where no account can be read; the stand-in last), and the fields of a format spec: canvas, slide types and their anatomy, density, register, caption conventions, what the account never does, fonts and colors, and how each field is measured, designed and used. | By the study skill before it writes the spec, included with it; by the build skill before it plans slides, through its link; by the design variations when they write a designed spec, linked from their handoff; by the research, through its link, for the spec's categories, canvas and frames. |

| [`account.md`](account.md) | What an account that keeps posting holds beside its posts, and where: the policy in the person's words (lane, follow-ups, window, ranking, sources, stance, voice, cadence, approval), each post's record with its states from idea to published, and the ledger of covered topics with what counts as an overlap. | By each skill only where the working folder is an account's, linked from the phase that reads or writes the record; a one-off post needs none of it. |

## Who reads this

[`instagram-card-news-format-study`](/design/skills/instagram-card-news-format-study/SKILL.md) includes `format-spec.md` and [`html-card-news-from-brief`](/design/skills/html-card-news-from-brief/SKILL.md) includes `topic-brief.md`, each declared in the skill's `skillcdn.include` by its root-relative path, so that it arrives with the skill wherever SkillCDN serves it; the build reads `format-spec.md` and [`web-card-news-topic-brief`](/product/skills/web-card-news-topic-brief/SKILL.md) reads `topic-brief.md` through a link at the phase that needs it, which keeps each skill within what a command prints at once; the set sits at the root because its skills are of three areas, design, product and marketing, and the format serves a page to all of them only from there. [`html-design-variations`](/design/skills/html-design-variations/SKILL.md) reads `format-spec.md` through the link in the variation set's handoff page, since only a card news run needs it, and the research reads it through its own link when a spec exists. `account.md` is linked, never included: `html-card-news-batch`, `instagram-post-scheduling` and the two skills above read it when they work in an account's folder, which `git-studio-setup` sets up as a repository. A copy of a skill taken into another agent, and an area's plugin, have none of the pages: each skill's Requirements say so and name this directory in the repository as the place to fetch them.

## What every card-news skill does the same way

- **The format spec is the only design, and a reference is measured, never copied.** A reference's slide types, density and register are measured, then reproduced or, at the user's pick, varied into a format of the user's own (format-spec.md "Where a spec comes from"); the build composes from the spec's types alone and carries no layouts of its own; a reference's photos, copy and name never enter a result.
- **Every sentence on a slide traces to a published sentence.** A number, a date, a quote or an interpretation comes from an article the agent read in full, cross-checked where two articles carry it; a frame's own caption counts as what the slide says only where it states a fact of the brief; what cannot be sourced is dropped or listed as unverified.
- **Every image shows the story and carries a credit.** Images and clips come from where the articles point, in order: the story's own video, the articles' photographs, the footage or post they cite, the subject's public pages, and an image search last; each is judged legible at phone size. The credit is the agency or the broadcaster and program, the photographer or the uploader where named, the outlet or channel it appeared in, the date; the caption's credit line is written from it.
- **The stance is the user's.** A post reports, comments or makes a side's case, calmly or aggressively, as the user or the account's policy says; the sourcing and the attribution hold whichever it is, and an unverified item never reaches a slide.
- **Making and posting are apart.** The research, the build and the batch deliver files and text and post nothing. Posting is the posting skill's job, for a post the person approved.
- **No paid tool.** A shell, a browser and the open web; a batch spends the host's tokens in proportion to its size and says so before it starts.

Each skill states these in its hard rules, in the form its workflow gives them, so that they hold where these pages do not travel; the design variations, which read no post and post nothing, state the one a look can break: a reference's photos, words and name never enter it.

## How these pages are written

Each page describes an artifact by its fields: what the field holds, how it is obtained, who reads it. A field a run found missing is added to the page and to the skill that writes it in the same change. Examples use placeholders (`example-account`, `example-outlet.example`, `0000-00-00`); no real account, outlet, story or person is named.
