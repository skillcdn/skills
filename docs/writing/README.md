# Writing: what every text shares

What the pack's skills hold in common when the result is words a person reads: a slide's sentence, the minutes of a meeting, a post's script, a brief, an answer with its sources. One page per topic, each stating a rule once in the form that holds for any text. The sharper form for one medium stays with the skill that makes it, and wins there: read the page a text needs before writing it, then the medium's own list.

| Document | Holds | Read |
|---|---|---|
| [`readers.md`](readers.md) | Who reads the text and what they do with it, the language and the terms, the register and the voice, plain words. | Before the first sentence. |
| [`sentences.md`](sentences.md) | One idea per sentence, a lead and a plain sentence, titles and headings, what is cut, and rewriting. | While writing, and at the review. |
| [`facts.md`](facts.md) | Where the content comes from, a figure with its source, what is still to be confirmed, when a hedge is right, quotes and claims, dates and names. | For every sentence that states a fact. |
| [`outside-readers.md`](outside-readers.md) | What stays inside the organization and where it goes instead, how tools and products are named, another party's words. | For a text that leaves the organization, before it is planned. |
| [`house-style.md`](house-style.md) | The characters and heading forms an organization bans in documents that leave it: the usual set, the question, writing within it, where the answer is kept, how the deck and the minutes helpers check it. | For a text that leaves the organization, from the first draft. |

## What is kept elsewhere

One topic has one page, and these are not repeated here.

- **Text that reads as machine-written.** [`/docs/variation/not-ai.md`](/docs/variation/not-ai.md) "Signals in words" is the pack's one list of the signals, with the fix for each and the tests a text passes.
- **Lines that are spoken.** [`/docs/higgsfield/pronunciation.md`](/docs/higgsfield/pronunciation.md) holds what a generated voice does with a line, and the script pages of the skills that make video hold the lines' own rules.
- **How a run speaks to its user.** The questions, the checkpoints and the delivery are rules of the repository's manifest ([`/SKILLCDN.md`](/SKILLCDN.md)), handed over with every skill.
- **A README and a working agreement.** [`/engineering/docs/git/readme-guide.md`](/engineering/docs/git/readme-guide.md) and [`/engineering/docs/git/agreement.md`](/engineering/docs/git/agreement.md).

## Each medium's own list

| The text is | Its own list is in |
|---|---|
| A slide of a deck | `html-deck-from-outline`: the deck's spec, its two registers and its reviewer's notes |
| The minutes of a meeting | `html-meeting-minutes-from-notes`: the composition rules and the review notes |
| A card news slide and its caption | `html-card-news-from-brief`: the script page, checked sentence by sentence against the format's spec |
| A sourced brief | `web-card-news-topic-brief`: the facts table and the angle |
| An answer from documents | `skillcdn-document-answer`: the answer's shape and its citations |
| A spoken line of an ad or a drama | `higgsfield-shorts-ad` and `higgsfield-shorts-drama`: the ad's story page and the drama's episode script page |
| The script of a news report an anchor reads | `web-news-anchor-report-script`: the script page (one hearing, one job a sentence, links the sources state), the spoken page (short sentences as an anchor says them, how each thing is said to be known, dates as the viewer lives them) and the cold listen |
| A research report | `meta-ad-library-creative-research` and `meta-ad-library-rising-products`: the report's order and its words |

A rule that turns out to hold for every medium moves here, into the sentence it bears on; a rule of one medium stays in its skill.
