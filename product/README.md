Skills for product planning and research: discovery (what is taking off in a market), research synthesis, a sourced brief of a story, concept directions and alternatives, requirements, specifications, roadmaps, prioritization. The area manifest next to this file ([SKILLCDN.md](SKILLCDN.md)) says who these skills are for and adds the rules every product skill follows on top of the repository's. The area mounts alone at `skillcdn.ai/gh/skillcdn/skills/product` and installs as the `product` plugin of the repository's Claude Code marketplace.

| Skill | Tool family | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [`skills/web-concept-variations/`](skills/web-concept-variations/) | Web | Four to six concept directions that get a reference's effect by other means, for a product, a post, a page, a deck or a campaign, from a reference the user gives or one found among what is working now. Read the reference for its intent, effect, aesthetics, fun, expected effect and the prior knowledge it rests on, find other references that get the same effect on the open web, place candidates on the concept axes with one near and one far, judge each for the AI feel before it is shown, compare them on one sheet with a recommendation and a runner-up, and hand the user's pick to the skill that produces. Nothing is spent. |
| [`skills/meta-ad-library-rising-products/`](skills/meta-ad-library-rising-products/) | Meta Ad Library | What advertisers are putting the most new advertising behind in a sector or a field, read from the public library with a browser: items, each one seller's (goods by default; apps, tools, services or courses where the sector is made of them), and the themes several sellers share. Group ads by seller, count each entry's active ads and those started in the last 7, 30 and 90 days, class it as a breakout, a new push or proven with its pace this week, and deliver a ranked report with the ads, the sellers, the offer and the risks, with a snapshot the next run measures real growth against. |
| [`skills/web-card-news-topic-brief/`](skills/web-card-news-topic-brief/) | Web | This week's candidate stories in the categories an account covers, the user's pick turned into a topic brief: every fact with the sentence of an article read in full, the angle as a published interpretation quoted from its outlet, numbers cross-checked across articles, the unverified items listed, and the images that show the story collected from where the articles point (their photos, the clip or footage they cite as frames, the subject's public pages, a search last), each with its caption, credit, pixel size, legibility and licence terms. |

Documents for this area go in `docs/` next to this file, where SkillCDN discovers them by default; there are none yet. The concept skill reads what every variation skill shares from the repository's [`docs/variation/`](../docs/variation/README.md); the research skill reads the shape of the brief it writes from the repository's [`docs/card-news/`](../docs/card-news/README.md), shared with the card news build in `design/`, and how a page is read in full from [`docs/browser/`](../docs/browser/README.md); the rising products skill reads what the two Meta Ad Library skills share from [`docs/meta-ad-library/`](../docs/meta-ad-library/README.md), the other being the creative research in `marketing/`.

How to write a skill: [guide/skill-authoring.md](../guide/skill-authoring.md). How to add one here: [guide/adding.md](../guide/adding.md). The `node scripts/check.mjs` check fails when a skill directory is missing from this table.
